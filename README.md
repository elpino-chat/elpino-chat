## Elpino is an AI support agent that runs customer support on autopilot

Customer support is full of the same questions: pricing, refunds, shipping, "where is my order?". They arrive at night, on weekends and on holidays, and they pull your team away from work that needs a person.

Elpino reads your public website, learns your business, and answers those questions for you, inside a chat widget on your site. When it isn't sure, or when a question needs a human, it brings your team into the conversation with the full history attached.

> **Try it now, with no signup:** enter your website at [elpino.chat/demo](https://elpino.chat/demo) and chat with an agent that has read your own pages.

## The problem we solve

| The problem | How Elpino handles it |
| --- | --- |
| **Support eats your team's time.** The same ten questions, asked a hundred different ways. | Elpino answers the routine, repeated questions that have clear answers, so people handle the rest. |
| **Most AI bots make things up.** A confident wrong answer about a refund policy is worse than no answer. | Answers come only from knowledge you approve: your pages, documents and notes. When Elpino is unsure, it says so and hands off. |
| **Setup takes weeks.** Training a bot, writing flows, maintaining intents. | Paste your website. Elpino reads your public pages in about a minute. No flows to build. |
| **AI and humans live in different tools.** Handoffs lose context. | One shared inbox for the AI and your team. Every AI answer is visible, and a person can hand a chat back to the AI. |
| **Seat pricing punishes growth.** Every new teammate costs more. | Unlimited seats on every plan, including Free. Handing a conversation to a human never costs extra. |
| **AI near customer data feels risky.** | Private details are swapped out before the AI sees them, conversations are never used to train models, and customers are verified before the AI acts. |

## How it works

1. **Enter your website.** Type your address. Elpino only reads public pages, the same ones a visitor can open.
2. **Elpino learns your pages.** It reads your pricing, FAQs and contact details, usually in under a minute. You can add PDFs, Word documents and pages you write yourself.
3. **Chat with your agent.** The real widget answers on a preview of your site. When you're happy, add one line of code, or the WordPress plugin, and it goes live.

When a question needs a person, the conversation lands in the shared inbox with the whole chat, the visitor's context and a clear owner.

## What you get

### AI answers you can trust
- **Answers from your own knowledge.** Elpino teaches itself from your website and sitemap, PDFs, Word documents, and pages you write. Answers come only from the knowledge you approve.
- **An answer audit trail.** See how each answer was produced and what it was based on.
- **Customer memory.** The agent remembers who it is talking to and keeps customer context across conversations.

### A shared inbox for your AI and your team
- **One inbox for both.** Team-wide "Join" alerts, clear ownership, and visitor context in every conversation.
- **Human handoff.** When the AI isn't sure, or a person should take over, the chat moves to your team with its full history.
- **Hand back to the AI.** Once a person is done, the conversation can return to the agent.
- **Tickets.** Turn a conversation into a ticket. Tickets go to Trello or Asana if you connect them, and otherwise live on your Issues page.

### An agent that can act, within limits you set
- **Orders (Shopify and WooCommerce).** For a verified, signed-in customer, the agent can find an order, show its status and tracking, and handle the simple fixes.
- **Payments (Stripe, Razorpay, Cashfree, Paystack).** Look up a payment, transaction or refund from inside a conversation.
- **Leads (HubSpot).** Capture a lead from the chat straight into your CRM.
- **Your own systems through MCP.** Connect your own MCP servers so the agent can call your tools.
- **Verified customers.** The agent confirms who a customer is before it acts on their account, using identity verification you control.

### Know what is happening
- **Analytics.** See real visitors, separated from bots, and how your AI is performing.
- **Customer profiles.** Build up context on the people who talk to you.

### Install it anywhere
- **One line of code.** Add the Elpino script to any site.
- **WordPress plugin.** Connect in one click, with no code.
- **Team connections.** Connect your team's Slack, Discord or Telegram from onboarding.

## Security and privacy

- **Private details are swapped out before the AI sees them.** The model works with short-lived references, not raw emails, internal IDs or amounts.
- **Conversations are never used to train models.**
- **Every customer is verified before the AI acts** on their account.
- **Order actions** use short-lived references and only run for a verified customer.
- **Your data stays scoped to your workspace.**

Read more in the [Security guide](https://elpino.chat/security-guide), the [Trust page](https://elpino.chat/trust) and the [security docs](https://elpino.chat/docs/security).

## Getting started

### Elpino Cloud (recommended)

1. Open [elpino.chat/demo](https://elpino.chat/demo) to try it on your own site, with no signup.
2. [Create your workspace](https://elpino.chat/signup). The Free plan needs no credit card.
3. Add your site key to your pages:

```html
<script src="https://cdn.elpino.chat/tag.js" data-site-key="YOUR_SITE_KEY" async></script>
```

Using WordPress? Install the [Elpino Chat plugin](https://github.com/elpino-chat/wordpress-plugin) instead and connect in one click.

### Self-hosting

The website, dashboard and chat widget in [`elpino_web`](https://github.com/elpino-chat/elpino_web) are open source and you can run them yourself. They talk to an Elpino backend, which is not open source, so a fully self-hosted setup is not available today. See that repository's README for configuration.
